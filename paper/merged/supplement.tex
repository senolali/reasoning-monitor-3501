\noindent\textbf{Purpose and evidence labels.}
This compact supplement records details needed to audit the main claims. Full
protocols, generated tables, trajectories, hash-checked derived data and scripts
are in the accompanying reproducibility archive. Results are labelled
confirmatory when frozen before data, prespecified secondary when frozen but not
primary, exploratory when rules preceded computation but the data were already
seen, and post-hoc otherwise.

\section{Study 1: LIC-RQ-v2}

\subsection{Task audit, transport and parsing}
The public math subset contains 103 tasks and 585 shards. Before model calls, 11
tasks matching inputs from earlier experiments and eight tasks whose sharding
altered meaning were excluded; two overlapped, leaving 17 unique exclusions.
Hash selection chose \nTasks{} untouched science tasks (4--9 turns; median 6)
without model output. Four disjoint tasks were used only for integration.

Both arms received a packed JSON transcript in one user message. The fresh arm
contained user shards only; the history arm interleaved the same shards with
earlier primary replies. Every cell used $K=\Krep{}$ same-context samples and
$L=\Lpar{}$ frozen instruction rewordings; only the primary response entered
later history. The semantic parser required an explicit final-answer or
need-more-information marker while accepting numbering, units and minor status
variants. Six empty Mistral-7B primaries were retained as invalid, not retried,
and transported using a fixed placeholder.

\subsection{Pre-final metrics and CRL}

SS were tested by task-clustered partial correlation with final failure,
adjusting for model, turn, revealed-information fraction and log context length.
No metric survived Holm correction (Table~\ref{tab:s1metrics}).

\section{Study 2: CDR-Stream}

\subsection{Generator and calibration}
Each 100-turn dialogue realizes state events and one exact-answer query from six
families: sum, difference, count-above-threshold, maximum, what-if and share.
Exact solvers verify every update, gold answer and paraphrase. A 12-level ladder
changes one complexity axis per level. Before stream generation, model-domain
mappings were fitted by isotonic regression and a one-sided trend test. A domain
was excluded unless adjacent regime gaps were at least 0.10, the easy-hard gap
at least 0.25, and the trend significant at $p\leq0.01$.

Models were selected before stream outcomes. Mistral-7B was at the floor, a
larger Gemma at the ceiling, and Llama-4 variants lacked repeat diversity;
GPT-4o-mini, Llama-3 70B and Gemma 3 12B passed the relevant gates. A capacity
probe confirmed that Llama-3 70B accepted and recalled the oldest message in a
full 100-turn packed history.

\subsection{Recorded deviations and missingness}
Two Llama-3 70B zero-truncation gates were overridden and recorded before the
main run: one of 1,440 calibration responses and two non-primary pilot responses
reached the 2,048-token ceiling. The replacement policy, frozen for all models,
kept truncated text, scored the cell missing, transported a truncated primary
as a placeholder and repeated inference with truncated cells scored incorrect.
The sensitivity analysis changed no result.

Funding paused acquisition after all 90 dialogue-history primary chains and the
full GPT-4o-mini sampling plan were complete. Llama and Gemma controls were then
completed under a separate frozen protocol. Their control-minus-history gaps
were tested by per-dialogue one-sided sign flips with Holm correction across the
two-model family; GPT-4o-mini was reported outside that family.

\subsection{Monitoring validation}
The $W=20$ detector mapping and threshold rule screened on GPT-4o-mini were
applied unchanged to unseen Llama and Gemma controls. Neither model passed the
single-stream screen; apparent LS leads also occurred frequently in stationary
dialogues. Six-dialogue CQ aggregation detected all three drift scenarios, but
Gemma produced one stationary-scenario alarm (Table~\ref{tab:validation}).

\section{Power and detector audit}

detector grids, and 300 drift streams per cell. Thresholds targeted 5\% false
alarms. Table~\ref{tab:power} shows that aggregation, rather than a different
single-stream score, is what moves observed 0.1--0.3 drops into a detectable
range.

The historical ADWIN comparator omitted the variance term of
\citet{bifet2007learning}; its bound exceeded every attainable mean difference
on streams of this length. Every affected analysis was rerun with the corrected
bound and CUSUM. No decision changed, and corrected ADWIN remained too
conservative for 100 turns. This audit illustrates why a generic comparator
must first be shown capable of alarming on the intended stream length.

\section{Reproducibility}
The release contains frozen protocols, configurations, analysis code, generated
tables and figures, derived reports and a machine-readable manifest. Raw
provider responses are excluded from the distributable archive. Key input and
report hashes are verified by the build script; no raw record is overwritten.
The version-controlled repository and archival release are identified in the
main article's data-availability statement.
